## Supplement after blind source audit

The disclosed results were 8/8 for each stream suite, 8/8 for both priority skill suites, and 7/8 for the priority baseline. The reported baseline survivor is `reprioritize-renumbers`. In the baseline's explicit tie scenario (lines 83–92), `old`, `new`, and `third` are reprioritized in that same order before any pop. An implementation that assigns a fresh admission order on every reprioritization can therefore still produce `old`, `new`, `third`. The seeded history also did not expose the reported survivor. A discriminating scenario must pop or otherwise compare an earlier reprioritized item against a tied competitor whose admission order has not also been refreshed before the observation.
