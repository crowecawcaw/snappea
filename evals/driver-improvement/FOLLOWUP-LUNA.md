# Smaller-model follow-up

After all six Sol submissions were frozen, source review found broad scenario
coverage in both arms. Initial returned grades show a ceiling on priority and
ownership, with some generated correct searches incomplete.

Repeat all three fixtures with six fresh Luna/high agents, one per report arm.
Keep tasks, tools, private faults, budgets and instructions unchanged. Reverse
the arm/letter mapping from Sol. No Sol sources or hidden feedback reach agents.
Preserve this as a separate exploratory cohort; do not pool model identities or
claim effect sizes. Assess whether narrower scenarios or instruction failures
appear with a smaller model and whether report content helps.

Do not remove the report-blind retry case or the successful ownership case.
No tooling fixes are applied during either cohort. Sol and Luna differ in both
model and thinking setting; a comparison between their cohorts is descriptive.
